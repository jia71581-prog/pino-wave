# Pre-outcome source-position protocol amendment

Date: 2026-08-13 UTC

No controlled-source model prediction, generated reference, score summary, or
method comparison existed when this amendment was made. The protocol remains a
fixed-19-Hz source-position study; source-frequency generalization and frequency
sweeps remain outside its scope.

The quantitative velocity panel was enlarged from five input-only selected
validation slices to the complete census of all 30 validation Marmousi velocity
slices. Each slice retains the same eight preregistered source positions, giving
240 repeated position--medium cases. The rank-15 slice remains the locked
qualitative panel. The change was made because five independent slices permit
only 32 sign patterns, so the minimum exact two-sided paired sign-flip p value is
0.0625. The enlarged census supports slice-level paired inference without
treating the eight positions within a slice as independent replicates.

Primary paired accuracy endpoints are complete-transient relative L2,
late-window relative L2, and high-spatial-band relative L2. Positive oriented
effects favour the proposed method. Inference averages the eight positions
within each velocity slice, resamples complete slices for confidence intervals,
uses paired slice-level sign flips, and applies Holm correction to the three
primary endpoints. Interpolation and outside-training-range positions must both
retain the favourable direction.
